Our Story

Where Tamil Heritage Meets Modern Luxury

The Vision

Couture Arangam was founded with a singular vision – to bring the rich textile heritage of Tamil Nadu to the global stage. Inspired by the centuries-old traditions of handloom weaving, intricate designs, and the legacy of Tamil craftsmanship, we create luxury fashion that celebrates cultural roots while embracing contemporary elegance.

Meet the Founder

Abhinav, the visionary behind Couture Arangam, grew up immersed in Tamil Nadu’s rich textile traditions. With a passion for fashion and cultural preservation, he embarked on a journey to craft a brand that blends timeless artistry with modern sophistication.

Honoring Tamil Textile Heritage

Every piece in our collection tells a story of tradition and innovation. From the opulent weaves of Kanjeevaram silk to the vibrant hues of Madras checks, our designs are a tribute to Tamil Nadu’s textile legacy.

The Future of Tamil Fashion

At Couture Arangam, we believe in sustainable luxury. By working closely with local artisans and embracing ethical production methods, we are shaping a future where Tamil culture continues to inspire global fashion.
